Privacy policy

Draft for review — last updated 25 October 2026. This draft describes current practice; it has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Who we are

MeritNama is an independent, non-official platform for analysing postgraduate medical induction data. Contact: itskaero@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details you provide to sign in, such as your email address and access credentials.
  • Sign-in and access records, including time, browser user agent and IP address, used to protect accounts and investigate misuse.
  • Content you create in the app, such as draft preference lists, saved scenarios and community posts.
  • Published induction records (for example merit-list entries) obtained from official publications, used to show you your own record and to compute aggregate statistics.

How we use it

  • To provide the features you use, including matching your account to your published record.
  • To produce aggregate, non-identifying statistics shown publicly.
  • To keep the service secure and fix errors.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your data

Your saved plans and scenarios are visible only to your account. Administrators can access records where needed to operate the service, correct data or investigate misuse; such access is logged.

Retention and your choices

You can ask us to export or delete your account data by email. Some access records are kept for a limited period for security purposes.

Public website

This public site does not set tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and may read a small public configuration document to display announcements.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and note the date above.
