The story so far

Every induction starts with a list. This one starts with you.

Chapter 1 · The list

A PDF decides the next five years.

Each induction round ends the same way. A merit list lands as a long PDF, thousands of names deep, and every doctor scrolls for one row: their rank, their score, and the seat it bought.

Chapter 2 · The question

“Will my score get me there?”

The answer was always out there, scattered across years of lists, gazette seat counts and forum threads. Every round, thousands of people asked the same question and started from zero.

Chapter 3 · The archive

So we kept every list.

meritNAMA gathered closing merit for every specialty and hospital, round by round, and cleaned the names so the years line up. A cutoff stopped being a rumour and became a trend you can read.

Chapter 4 · The rebuild

Then we started again, properly.

The new meritNAMA is a private app with real sign-in, a design system that works on any phone, and a preference planner that checks your ordered choices against closing merit before you submit them.

Chapter 5 · You

Plan with the history in your hand.

Open your rank, see where it has landed before, and order your preferences with your eyes open. The beam on this page climbs as each piece ships. When it reaches the top, the new meritNAMA opens at meritnama.online.
