Questions

Asked most often

Something else? Copy the address at the bottom of the page and write to us.

Is meritNAMA an official admissions site?

No. meritNAMA is an independent analysis of Punjab postgraduate induction. Always confirm decisions against the official merit lists and notifications.

Where does the data come from?

From published merit lists for each induction round and the gazette notifications that set seat counts. Names are cleaned so years can be compared.

Can I keep using the current version?

Yes. It stays available until the new site opens.

When does the new meritNAMA open?

When the beam reaches 100%. It is 65% built today, and it will open at meritnama.online.

Independent

meritNAMA is not an official admissions authority and is not affiliated with any examining or inducting body. Official merit lists and notifications always take precedence.

Data

Figures come from published merit lists and gazette notifications. Projections and planner results are estimates based on past rounds, not guarantees of placement.
