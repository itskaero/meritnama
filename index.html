Questions

What people ask us.

Is meritNAMA an official induction portal?

No. meritNAMA is independent and not part of PHF, CPSP, the Health Department or any admissions authority. Applications, consent and results happen only through the official channels.

Does an estimate mean I will get that seat?

No. Estimates use the data we have and stated assumptions. Real results depend on official lists, consent, joining and corrections that an estimate cannot know in advance.

What happened to the previous site?

It still works. Use Open the previous version at the top of this page. New features are added to the new site only.

Who can see my preference lists?

Only you. meritNAMA never publishes a candidate's plans. The privacy policy has the details.

I found an error. How do I report it?

Email itskaero@gmail.com with the induction, the round and a link to the official document. We check every report against the source.
