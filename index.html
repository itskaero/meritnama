Good questions

Is meritNAMA an official admissions site?

No. meritNAMA is an independent analysis of Punjab postgraduate induction. Always confirm decisions against the official merit lists and notifications.

Where does the data come from?

From published merit lists for each induction round and the gazette notifications that set seat counts. Hospital and specialty names are matched so years can be compared.

Will I need a new account?

Yes. You sign in with a link sent to your email, or with Google. To see your own merit record, you verify the email address on that record once.

Can I keep using the current version?

Yes. It stays available until the new site opens.

When does the new site open?

When the rebuild reaches 100. It is 65 out of 100 today, and it will open at meritnama.online.
